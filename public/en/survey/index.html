The service

Customer insight, from the source.

Published research tells you how a market is structured. Customers tell you why they buy, what they pay and what they would change. Kira Survey puts that conversation within reach for companies entering or expanding across Southeast Asia, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Australia and New Zealand.

Every study starts from the decision you need to make, so the questions, the respondents and the reporting are built around it.

Local perspectives for smarter decisions.

Methods

Two ways to hear from customers.

Customer surveys

Structured questionnaires to a defined group of consumers, business buyers or channel partners, in the local language, for results you can compare across segments and markets.

In-depth interviews

One-to-one conversations with customers, buyers and decision-makers that explore the reasons behind behaviour, in the respondent's own words.

Combined studies

Interviews to find the right questions, then a survey to measure how widely the answers hold; or the other way round.

How it works

From question to customer voice.

01

Share your question

Tell us the market, the customers you want to hear from and the decision you are weighing. A short brief is enough to begin.

02

We design the study

We propose the questionnaire or interview guide and the respondent profile, and you approve them before fieldwork starts.

03

We run it in the market

We reach respondents who match the profile and carry out the survey or interviews in the local language.

04

You receive the findings

A written report with the evidence behind each finding. Interview summaries are available on request.

Typical questions

Where asking customers directly matters most.

Who really decides

Who influences and who signs off on a purchase, and what they look for before they choose.

Price and willingness to pay

Where customers see value, how they react to price points, and what they pay today.

Product and proposition fit

How customers respond to your product, brand or concept, and what would have to change for this market.

Channels and the purchase journey

How customers find, compare and buy, and which channels they trust.

Unmet needs

What customers still cannot get from today's suppliers, and where the opening is.
