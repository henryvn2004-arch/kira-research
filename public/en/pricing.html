— Questions

Before you join.

What is the waitlist?
A list of teams who want the subscription when it opens. Telling us which plan you need helps us decide what to build first. Joining does not commit you to anything.
Will I be charged when I join?
No. Nothing is charged now and we do not ask for payment details. We will email you before anything is charged, with the final terms.
What can I read without a plan?
The executive summary of every report: the overview, selected data tables, the table of contents and the glossary. No account is needed.
How do PDF downloads work?
Online reading has no limit. Downloading a report as PDF uses your plan's allowance: 5 during a Week plan, 20 a month on Month, 30 a month on Annual.
Which markets and industries do you cover?
Southeast Asia, Australia, New Zealand and Taiwan, in industries where foreign companies sell into the local market. New reports are added as they are published. The library shows what is available today.
Can you research a question the library does not cover?
Custom research is paused for now. Tell us what you need through the contact form. It helps us decide what to cover next.
