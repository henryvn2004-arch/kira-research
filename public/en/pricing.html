— Questions

Before you buy.

Can I read something before I buy?
Yes. Every report has a free preview of its key pages and its full table of contents, every transcript opens with its summary and the first part of the interview, and every company list has a free overview. No account is needed to read them.
Can’t I get this from a chatbot?
You can get a fast first answer from a search assistant, and it is a fine way to start. A report is for when the answer has to hold up in a meeting: each figure is tied to a dated source you can open, the document does not change from one day to the next, and it ends with a recommendation and the conditions that would change it. We do not claim to replace your own judgement, only to give you something solid to put it on.
How are your reports made?
They are desk research from public and licensed sources, with a source attached to every figure, reviewed before publication. We say plainly what each report is built on and where it stops. The methodology page explains each step.
Why not a large research firm?
A large firm is the right choice for primary research, a bespoke scope or an advisory relationship, and it prices accordingly. Our reports answer one defined decision at a fixed, published price, are available now rather than in weeks, and come in English, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
What if I cannot open or download it?
If you paid and cannot open or download what you bought, the file is damaged or has pages missing, you received the wrong language edition, or you were charged twice, we refund you in full within 7 days. Write to hello@kiraresearch.com and we reply within 2 business days. Because the free preview lets you check what you are buying, we do not refund for a change of mind. Nothing here limits your rights under the consumer law where you live.
How often are reports updated?
Each report is refreshed within 12 months of its publication, and every report shows its date. A report’s price steps down as it ages, so an older report costs less than a new one of the same size.
Which markets and sectors are covered?
Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar and Brunei, together with Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Australia and New Zealand, in sectors where companies compete for local demand. New reports are added continuously, and the library shows what is published today.
