Transcript

01How business is going

KAKIRA Analyst

Thanks for making time. How is the diesel oil business this year compared with the last two?

EXExpert

Honestly, flatter than we'd like on the new-truck side. Gaikindo, the dealers' association, counted 59,303 trucks sold last year, down from 66,570 the year before. 1 And that number misses a lot. Many of the new Chinese trucks go straight to the mine sites and never show up in those figures, so when people say the truck market is shrinking, I'd say the part we can see is shrinking. 1

KAKIRA Analyst

So what's holding volume up?

EXExpert

Coal. Production hit a record 830.48 million tonnes in 2024, well over the 710 million target, and about half of it went to export. 2 Mining is still the biggest off-road user of diesel oil, then construction, then the plantations. 3 My on-road customers are mostly here in Jabodetabek, because this is where most fleets bring their trucks in for service. 14

02Who buys, and how they decide

KAKIRA Analyst

Walk me through your customers. Who buys diesel oil from you?

EXExpert

Two very different worlds. First the fleets. They buy straight from us, the distributor, and they tell you why without being asked: a better price, a guarantee that what's in the drum is genuine, and service. 4 The service part is real work. We take oil samples and test them for free, so the fleet manager can see when the oil actually needs changing instead of guessing. 5

Then the owner-drivers. Most of them buy the cheapest oil they can find, and a lot of the time that means fake oil. 6 But you also meet the other kind, the man who owns one truck and feeds his family with it. He buys good oil and he won't argue about the price. 6

KAKIRA Analyst

How does an owner-driver decide when to change oil, and which oil?

EXExpert

He asks his mechanic. The bengkel sets the rhythm, how often and how many litres, and most drivers just follow it. 7 So if you want the owner-driver, you don't talk to the owner-driver. You talk to the mechanic. 7

03Biodiesel: B40 now, B50 from July

KAKIRA Analyst

Biodiesel blends keep going up. Do your fleet customers feel it?

EXExpert

They complain about it every visit. Aptrindo, the truckers' association, said it plainly: on B40 the trucks burn a bit more fuel, lose some power, and need the fuel filter changed and the tank drained more often. 9 Now B50 started on 1 July 2026, and the mining companies were already worried about the extra cost before it began. 10

KAKIRA Analyst

Does that change anything for an oil distributor?

EXExpert

It gives you a reason to sit down with the fleet manager. When he is already opening up the engine more often, he wants to know what the new fuel is doing inside it, and that's exactly what the free oil test shows him. 9 5

04Fakes and grey imports

KAKIRA Analyst

Is counterfeit oil still a problem in Jakarta, or is it mostly an outer-island issue now?

EXExpert

It's right here. In July 2025 the West Jakarta police arrested three people in Kembangan who were selling fake famous-brand oil around Jakarta, Tangerang and Bekasi. 15 Then in September they raided a place in Cengkareng where used oil was being cleaned with chemicals until it looked clear, then packed with copied caps, stickers and even barcodes of the leading local brand. They sold it through a social-media marketplace and by word of mouth. 8

So the old advice, check the cap and the seal, doesn't protect anyone anymore. 8 Outside Java it's worse. Sumatra and Kalimantan have always had the most fake and grey product, coming in by sea from Singapore and overland from East Malaysia. 16 That is the whole reason a serious fleet buys from a distributor: we guarantee what's in the drum. 4

05Rules on the road and on the label

KAKIRA Analyst

What regulation matters most to your business right now?

EXExpert

Two things. On the product side, diesel engine oil has to carry the SNI mark under the Ministry of Industry's regulation 25/2018. Anything without it is supposed to come off the shelf, and imported product gets sent back. 13 On the truck side, everyone is watching ODOL. Parliament, the government and the drivers' associations agreed in August 2025 to aim for zero overloaded trucks in 2027, and a joint team is still writing the details. 11

06How the brands compete for the channel

KAKIRA Analyst

How do the oil brands go to market through houses like yours?

EXExpert

Pertamina is the reference for everyone, on price and on being available everywhere, and each of its distributors is tied to its own region. 17 Almost all diesel oil goes through distributors anyway, not straight from the oil companies. 18 The international brands that did well went after the big fleets with supply contracts. The others went into retail and fought for the shop shelf. 12

KAKIRA Analyst

Is the fleet mix changing?

EXExpert

It's getting heavier. Cheap second-hand heavy trucks from China pushed a lot of transporters to buy bigger. 19 And now there are the new Chinese trucks going into the mines as well. 1

07Advice to a new brand

KAKIRA Analyst

If a Japanese or Korean oil brand asked you how to get into this market, what would you tell them?

EXExpert

Start with the fleets, with a supply contract, not with advertising. 12 Give them the testing service so the fleet manager can see the difference in his own engines. 5 And make it easy to prove the oil is genuine. The SNI mark is the minimum, because the fakes now copy everything else. 13 8

KAKIRA Analyst

Last question. Easy market or hard market?

EXExpert

Hard. The leader is cheap and it's everywhere. 17 Most owner-drivers will always buy on price. 6 But the fleets pay for genuine oil and for service, and that's where a new brand can win. 4
