Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

KIRA RESEARCH ("we") publishes market research on Southeast Asia. This page explains what we collect when you use kiraresearch.com, why, who helps us handle it, and how to ask us to stop or delete it. We collect only what the features below need.

What we collect, and why

WhenWhatWhy
You ask to be told when a report is publishedYour email, the report or keyword you asked about, your languageTo email you once when it is ready, and to see which reports readers want.
You join the subscription waitlist or send an enquiryYour email and what you type in the form (name, company, role, message, plan)To reply to you and to plan our products.
You create an account or buy a reportYour email and sign-in details; the report, amount and date of the purchase. Card details go to PayPal and never reach us.To give you access to what you bought and to send a receipt.
You submit any formYour IP address and browser type, kept with the requestTo stop spam and abuse.
You visit the siteOur own visit counter stores a random per-tab number, the page path and a coarse source (for example "google"). No cookies, no IP address, no email. Entries older than 180 days are deleted. It is switched off if your browser sends "Do Not Track".To see how many people read what, and which pages lead to a sign-up.
You visit the site (Google Analytics)Google Analytics 4 sets cookies and records pages viewed, device and approximate location.The same, with Google's reporting tools.
You arrive from one of our Google adsThe ad click ID and campaign tags in the link are kept in your browser for 90 days and saved with a sign-up you make, so we can tell which ad worked. Skipped if your browser sends "Do Not Track".To measure and improve our advertising.
You search the libraryThe words you typed. When a search finds no report, the words may be sent to an AI service to file it under an industry. We do not send your email with it.To show you a matching coming-soon page and to decide which reports to write.
We email youWhether the email was delivered, bounced or was marked as spam. Addresses that bounce or complain are not emailed again.To keep our email reaching people and to respect a complaint.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to profile you for anyone else.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase — database and sign-in (servers in Australia).
  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • Resend — sends our emails.
  • PayPal — takes payments.
  • Google — Analytics and Ads.
  • An AI provider — reads the search words described above, nothing else.

These companies may process data in other countries. They act for us and under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

Visit-counter entries: 180 days. Everything else: for as long as we need it for the purpose above or the law requires, and until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to stop emailing you: reply to any email we sent you and say so.
  • Turn on "Do Not Track" in your browser to switch off our visit counter and ad-click memory.
  • Block or clear cookies and site data in your browser at any time. Opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, we update this page and its date.
