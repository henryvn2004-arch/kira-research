ABOUT

Market-entry research for the people investors ask first.

When a foreign company looks at an ASEAN market, its first questions go to an industrial park, a law or accounting firm, a bank, a consultant or an investment-promotion agency. We write the research those teams need to answer.

— What we do

Questions first, then the market.

Each report starts from a question a foreign investor asks at a given stage. Is this market worth a look? How do we get in? How do we grow once we are there?

We cover Southeast Asia, Australia, New Zealand and Taiwan. We focus on industries where foreign companies come to sell into the local market, not on export-only manufacturing.

Our reports are desk research from public and licensed sources. Every figure carries its source, and our research team reviews each report before it is published.

What we are

  • A research house. We write and publish market-entry research. Our work is the reports, not software.
  • A subscription library. One subscription opens the full library for online reading, with a monthly PDF allowance. We are opening with a waitlist.
  • Published in the investor's language. English, Japanese and Korean today, with Simplified Chinese being added, so a report can go straight to the investor.

What we are not

  • Not a fieldwork agency. We do not run surveys, interviews or store checks. We say so in every methodology note.
  • Not a generic market-size database. Each report answers a specific entry question for a specific market, with the reasoning shown.
  • Not a custom research shop, for now. Commissioned projects are paused. Tell us what is missing and it helps us decide what to cover next.
