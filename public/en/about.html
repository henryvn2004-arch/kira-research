ABOUT

Clear evidence for decisions in Southeast Asia.

KIRA Research publishes research on the markets, sectors and entry routes of Southeast Asia, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Australia and New Zealand. Each report is written to make one investment or entry decision better informed.

— What we do

The decision first, then the market.

Each report begins with the decision it is meant to inform: whether a market is worth pursuing, how to enter it, or where to grow once established.

We cover Southeast Asia, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Australia and New Zealand, and concentrate on sectors where companies compete for local demand rather than on export-only manufacturing.

Our reports are desk research from public and licensed sources. Every figure carries its source, and our research team reviews each report before it is published.

What we are

  • A research house. We research, write and publish. The analysis is the product.
  • A subscription library. One subscription opens the full library for online reading, with an allowance of PDF editions. Subscriptions open soon; early access is by request.
  • Published in the reader's language. English, Japanese and Korean, with Simplified Chinese being added. Figures and sources are identical across editions.

What we are not

  • Not a fieldwork agency. Our reports are built from published and licensed sources, not from surveys or store checks. First-hand perspective is available separately through Kira Experts.
  • Not a market-size database. Each report addresses a specific question in a specific market, with the reasoning shown.
  • Not a consulting firm. Our work is published research. When a question calls for first-hand experience, Kira Experts arranges a conversation with someone who has it.
