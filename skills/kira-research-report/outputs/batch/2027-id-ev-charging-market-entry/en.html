How we built this view

This brief asks how a Japanese, Korean or Chinese charging operator should enter Indonesia, and what the first year costs. It rests on two evidence streams: rules and tariffs (investment and licensing rules, the regulated charging price, PLN's bulk power terms) and market evidence (car sales, charger counts, session data, company moves). Every quantitative claim carries an explicit source tag.

Rules and tariffs

→ Investment and licensing rules
The risk-based licensing regulation PP 28/2025, the business code for charging stations, and the energy ministry's simplified registration, read through official statements and legal briefings.
→ Regulated price and power cost
The capped selling price per kWh, the capped service fee per session, and PLN's bulk tariff and connection discount for charging operators.
→ Our reading, flagged
Where a rule is stated by officials but not yet published as a procedure, we say so. This brief is market research, not legal advice.

Market evidence

→ Car sales and charger counts
Industry-association wholesale data for battery-electric cars, and the public charger stock by speed and province from PLN and the energy ministry.
→ Company moves, in four languages
Operator, carmaker, landlord and fleet announcements, searched in Indonesian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese as well as English.
→ Triangulated estimates
Sessions per charger, session margins, payback and the year-one budget combine published inputs with a method shown on each page. Tagged [Kira estimates].

Conditional go: enter with a few busy hubs in Greater Jakarta, not a wide network; year one costs about USD 2 million

The tension: Indonesia has one public charger for every 37 electric cars, yet the average Jakarta charger served only two to three sessions a day.

Source: [Kira estimates] from [Bisnis Apr 2026], [PLN Dec 2024], [Kepmen 182.K/2023], [PLN 2022 packages]; method on pages 11-12

Why the answer is a few hubs

The network is short of fast chargers, not of chargers. 4,778 public units served about 175,000 battery-electric cars at end-2025, but only one in four units is 50 kW or faster [ESDM Feb 2026]; [Kontan Jan 2026].

The price is capped, so volume decides the return. A 24 kWh ultra-fast session leaves about Rp 67,000 (USD 3.7); a charger needs about ten sessions a day to pay back in five years [Kira estimates].

The sites and the repeat customers sit with partners. PLN owns three in five public units; landlords, toll-road operators and taxi fleets hold the rest of the volume [PLN Nov 2025].

Set up a wholly owned operator, sign a taxi-fleet anchor and two landlords in Jakarta, and require ten sessions a day per fast charger before scaling.

Where the sessions are

Jakarta's chargers are getting busier. They logged 803,235 sessions in 2025 and 430,593 in the first quarter of 2026 alone, more than five times the same quarter a year earlier [Bisnis Apr 2026]. Per unit, that is about 2.0 sessions a day in 2025 and 3.3 in Q1 2026 [Kira estimates].

The toll roads peak at holidays. On the second day after Eid in March 2026, PLN chargers handled 18,088 sessions and 427,980 kWh, four times the year before [PLN Lebaran 2026].

How we derived itSessions per unit divide Jakarta's sessions by its average unit count over the period; a unit may carry more than one connector.
Start where usage is compounding: sessions per Jakarta unit rose from about 2.0 a day in 2025 to 3.3 in early 2026.

011 / 031

A 24 kWh ultra-fast session leaves about Rp 67,000, or USD 3.7, after power, landlord and payments

The capped service fee is half of what the driver pays and about 70% of what the operator keeps.

Money flow of one ultra-fast session
Indonesia · Rp thousand per session · 24 kWh at the capped price · excluding VAT · 2026 terms
Source: [PLN Dec 2024]; [Kepmen 182.K/2023]; 24 kWh per session, landlord and payment shares [Kira estimates]; Rp 17,950 per USD [BI Sep 2026]

What sets each layer

Price and fee are capped by the state. Operators may sell at up to Rp 2,466 per kWh and add at most Rp 25,000 per fast session or Rp 57,000 per ultra-fast session, before VAT [PLN Dec 2024]; [Kepmen 182.K/2023].

Power got cheaper in December 2024. PLN moved charging operators to a bulk tariff of Rp 1,100 per kWh, down from Rp 1,644, and halved the connection fee [PLN Dec 2024]. Sessions average about 24 kWh, from PLN's holiday data [Kira estimates]; [PLN Lebaran 2026].

ImplicationWithout the fee, the same session would leave about Rp 21,000. Any cut to the fee cap is the biggest single threat to the case.
Model every site on the capped fee and the bulk tariff, and negotiate landlord shares on margin, not on revenue, where possible.

012 / 031

A fast charger pays back in five years only at about ten sessions a day; Jakarta's average is two to three

Cheaper hardware lowers the bar to about six sessions a day, still nearly twice the early-2026 average.

Payback years for one 120 kW charger, by sessions a day
Indonesia · Rp 66,600 kept per session, Rp 30 m a year upkeep · two installed costs · axis cut at 25 years
Source: [Kira estimates] from [Bisnis Apr 2026], [PLN 2022 packages], [PLN Dec 2024], [Kepmen 182.K/2023]

How the curve is built

Each session keeps about Rp 66,600 after power, a 15% landlord share and 5% for payments (page 11). Upkeep is Rp 30 m a year per unit [Kira estimates].

Installed cost is the swing factor. PLN's 2022 partner package priced an outdoor charger above 100 kW at Rp 1.08 bn and a 50 kW unit at Rp 0.60 bn; imported hardware has fallen since, so we show Rp 1.0 bn and Rp 0.6 bn [PLN 2022 packages].

CaveatA 60 kW unit with the lower Rp 25,000 fee needs about the same ten sessions a day: the smaller fee offsets its lower cost.
Underwrite each fast charger at ten sessions a day, and buy hardware at 2026 import prices, not at the old partner-package prices.

Owning the operator keeps price and customer; the PLN route is cheaper but hands both to PLN

Seven ways in, placed by the capital they put at risk and the control they give over pricing, data and the driver relationship.

Why the top right

The PLN partnership is the lightest route, and the least controlled. PLN supplies the licence, power and app listing; the partner brings land or chargers and shares revenue [PLN 2022 packages]; [Emerhub 2026]. Pricing and the driver relationship stay with PLN.

A carmaker or fleet contract removes demand risk but not much else. Hyundai built its network with five local operators, and Xanh SM charges its own taxis at depots [S&P Jul 2024]; [Xanh SM 2024].

PrecedentTerra Charge launched in August 2024, worked with PLN and property firms for sites, and had 300+ units at 250 sites 14 months later [Terra Charge PR 2024]; [Terra Charge Oct 2025].
Own the operating company, and use PLN, carmakers and fleets as contracts around it rather than as the vehicle itself.

Year one costs about Rp 37 bn, or USD 2.1 million, and six-tenths of it is fast-charger hardware

A 20-site Jakarta pilot with 30 fast or ultra-fast chargers and 20 AC chargers, from company set-up to the first full year of operation.

Year-one budget for a 20-site pilot
Greater Jakarta · Rp bn · 2027 · excluding land, VAT and electricity (passed through to drivers)
Source: [Kira estimates]; hardware from [PLN 2022 packages] at 2026 discounts; connection discount [PLN Dec 2024]; Rp 17,950 per USD [BI Sep 2026]

What the budget assumes

Hardware dominates. Twenty 60 kW units at Rp 0.6 bn and ten 120 kW units at Rp 1.0 bn installed make Rp 22 bn; the spend alone clears the Rp 10 bn per-province investment floor [PLN 2022 packages]; [IBP Aug 2025].

Revenue covers little of year one. With chargers live for about six months at four sessions a day, sales reach about Rp 2-3 bn [Kira estimates].

RangeRp 30-45 bn (USD 1.7-2.5 m), depending on hardware prices, how many sites need new transformers, and team size.
Budget USD 2-2.5 million for year one, and buy chargers in two tranches so the second waits for the month-9 utilisation reading.
