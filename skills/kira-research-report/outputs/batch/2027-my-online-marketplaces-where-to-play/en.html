How we built this view

This view is built from two evidence streams: analyst research — English-, Malay- and Chinese-language desk research, platform and regulator disclosures, and analyst synthesis — and secondary anchors — official statistics, published regional datasets and consumer surveys, and triangulated estimates. Every quantitative claim carries an explicit source tag.

Analyst research

→ Local-language desk research
Malay- and Chinese-language press, ministry statements and seller notices — often the only place fee changes, rule pauses and enforcement dates are reported.
→ Analyst synthesis
KIRA in-house triangulation across multiple disclosed inputs. Tagged inline as [Kira estimates].
→ Platform and company disclosures
Listed-company results, platform announcements and seller-centre notices, read for direction rather than taken at face value.

Secondary anchors

→ Statistical and regulatory authorities
DOSM, Bank Negara Malaysia, the finance and domestic-trade ministries and the communications regulator — cited inline by named source.
→ Industry datasets and surveys
Regional e-commerce datasets and published consumer surveys, with sample and date stated where a figure depends on them.
→ Triangulated estimates
Where no single source covers a figure (platform shares, 2030 cases, segment scores), we combine 2-3 inputs and show the method. Tagged [Kira estimates].

Conditional go: Malaysia is worth entering through official brand stores and creators on two platforms, not by building another marketplace

The tension: online spend grew 21% in 2025, yet the share of Malaysians who shop online has stopped rising; the next ringgit comes from bigger baskets on the platforms that already hold the buyers.

Why the answer is conditional

The pool is real and still growing. E-commerce GMV reached about US$20 bn in 2025, up 21% [eConomy 2025], roughly a tenth of retail trade [Kira estimates].

Growth now comes from baskets, not new buyers. The share of adults buying online held at 60-62% between 2025 and 2026 [Survey Jul 2026]; internet use is near saturation at 98% [DataReportal 2026].

Two platforms hold the shelf. Shopee and TikTok Shop take about four ringgit in five [Kira estimates]; fees and new rules are rising at the same time.

Enter as a brand: official stores on Shopee and TikTok Shop, creator-led selling and local stock, with halal and SIRIM-ready products from day one.

SECTION 01

A US$20 bn market growing on bigger baskets

E-commerce reached about US$20 bn in 2025 and grew 21%. The 2025 jump came from new shoppers; the 2026 data say that wave is over, so the next leg rests on spend per shopper.

Chapter 1 · Market size
007 / 039

Goods sold online came to about RM83 bn in 2025, a tenth of retail trade; the larger official figures count services and business sales

Malaysia publishes several "e-commerce" numbers; they measure different things and should not be compared directly.

Four yardsticks for Malaysian online and retail spend
RM bn · latest full year · bars to scale
Source: [DOSM Feb 2026] and [Kira estimates] for retail trade; [DOSM Jun 2026]; [eConomy 2025]; [MOF LVG 2026] grossed up at the 10% rate [Kira estimates]

Which number to use

We size the market as goods sold online: about RM83 bn (US$20 bn) in 2025 [eConomy 2025], a tenth of retail trade sales [Kira estimates].

The official RM375 bn is a different scope. DOSM counts all B2C e-commerce income of firms, including travel, services and own websites, for 2024 [DOSM Jun 2026].

Growth differs by yardstick too: platform GMV rose 47.6% in 2025 [MW 2026] against 21% for all e-commerce [eConomy 2025], because platforms took share from brand sites and social chat selling.

Size any business case on the RM83 bn goods pool and its platform split, not on the RM375 bn headline.

The answer to the hook

011 / 039

Young shoppers pay later: pay-later spend rose 31% in six months to RM9.3 bn, at an average ticket of RM91

Payment habits shape the basket: instalments lift ticket size, and the platform that owns the pay-later product owns the checkout.

Who pays how

Pay-later is a young habit: the average ticket is RM91 and about 40% of users are 30 or younger [Parliament 2025]; active accounts rose from 5.1 m to 6.5 m in six months [RinggitPlus 2026].

Three providers dominate: Shopee, Grab and Atome handle about 97% of pay-later transactions [HitPay 2025].

Payments are already digital: e-payments reached 538 per person in 2025 and DuitNow QR doubled to 3.0 bn transactions [BNM 2026].

Price ladders around RM90-150 with instalment options; checkout financing sits with Shopee and Grab, so list where they operate.

The East Malaysia gap

Sabah and Sarawak hold nearly a fifth of residents [DOSM Pop 2025] [Kira estimates], but parcels go via Kota Kinabalu or Kuching and can need customs clearance [Retailer rates 2026].

Local stock cuts cost: fulfilment hubs in Kota Kinabalu and Kuching reduce delivery cost by about 20% [Sun Sep 2025].

Selangor is the core: with Kuala Lumpur it is the densest delivery zone and the natural first hub [Kira estimates].

Serve the peninsula from a Klang Valley hub; add East Malaysian stock only once demand there is proven.

The 10% low-value goods tax gives a rare official read on cheap imports sold online from abroad.

Reading the tax line

The tax has applied since 1 Jan 2024 to goods of RM500 or less bought from abroad; sellers above RM500,000 a year must register [Skrine 2023].

Collections rose 72% to RM817 m in 2025 [MOF LVG 2026]. Part of the jump is better compliance, so true import growth is lower [Kira estimates].

Cross-border low-price platforms such as Temu, in Malaysia since Sep 2023 [MW 2023], compete hardest in this band.

A brand shipping parcel by parcel from Japan or Korea competes inside the taxed import band; bulk-imported local stock delivers in days, not weeks.

Why TikTok Shop gains

Discovery happens in video. Live selling and creators turn browsing into buying; Ramadan 2026 sales rose over 130% [Malay Mail Apr 2026].

Support for sellers: a RM180 m package with the domestic-trade ministry cut commissions on essentials and added live hubs [Bernama Jun 2026].

Budget for creators and live selling from launch; a store without video content misses the platform that six in ten online shoppers now use.

How we built the cases
