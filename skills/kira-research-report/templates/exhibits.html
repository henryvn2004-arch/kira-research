Taxes take 22% of the shelf price, more than the whole distribution chain

Side-main layout: commentary leads on the left, the price build-up bridge on the right. Totals in dark grey, the insight bars in blue.

Reading the bridge

PHP 18.0 ex-factory becomes PHP 32.9 on shelf. Excise and VAT add PHP 7.1; distributors and wholesalers together add PHP 3.7.

The retailer keeps PHP 4.1, the largest single trade margin, because sari-sari stores sell by the bottle and carry credit risk.

ImplicationA 4% excise indexation in 2026 costs more per bottle than the wholesaler's entire margin.

Where the money is
