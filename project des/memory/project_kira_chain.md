## Direction change 2026-10-11: Kira Chain → "Kira List" (paused)

Owner decision: keep the name Kira Chain, but revamp the product into **searchable, downloadable lists** of local business partners: distributors, agents, partners, competitors, suppliers in a given area (e.g. a soft-drink maker such as URC searching "beverage distributors in Bình Dương"). User searches by their own product, not by a brand's distributors. The list is dynamic (built at search time), downloadable as Excel/PDF with a link per row, and shown on a map of the country so the distribution footprint is visible at a glance. Keep `/en/chain/` as is; only the product is revamped. The old to-do list (request-email sender, plan gating, analyst review of arrows) is dropped unless the owner brings it back.

**Status: PAUSED by the owner.** Owner is looking for another approach and will report back. Nothing in the repo was changed for this direction.

**What was tried (pilot, nothing committed):**
- Gemini's sample file (`Danh_Sach_300_NPP_Kho_Si_DrThanh_MienNam.xlsx`) is fabricated: names, phones and addresses are generated by formulas, not scraped. Do not reuse it.
- Google Places API (New) Text Search did return real data (HCMC, 13 of 22 districts: 233 rows after filtering, 85% with phone, 20% with website). But the Google Cloud project (`kira-research`, billing paid, VND) is hard-capped at **100 `searchText` requests/day** (429 `SearchTextRequestPerDayPerProject`, limit 100) while the Console shows 75,000. `searchNearby` was not capped in one test. Cause is not confirmed by Google; most likely **Vietnam is on Google Maps Platform's Prohibited Territories list** (verified in search results of Google's own page: China, Crimea, Cuba, Donetsk, Iran, Luhansk, North Korea, Syria, Vietnam). The Console also shows a "cannot be billed to accounts located in prohibited territories" dialog.
- Rejected workarounds (would hide the account's location, risk termination, and do not change the account-level cap): paying with a US PayPal/card, calling from a proxy server abroad. The only clean route found is asking Google in writing from a real US legal entity. Running within the cap over several days is possible (~100 requests/day, reset about 07:00 UTC) but still uses Places from a restricted account, so keep that data internal only.
- OpenStreetMap: sandbox cannot reach `overpass-api.de` or Geofabrik; mirrors returned HTTP 500 on real queries. One measurement: Chợ Lớn area, 300 `shop` features, 12% with phone.
- Overture Maps Places (release 2026-09-23.1, CDLA-Permissive-2.0, mostly Meta data) is reachable from the sandbox through DuckDB on anonymous S3 (unset `AWS_*` env, they are proxy-injected). Category `food_beverage_distributor` exists. Result for the 3 provinces: 177 usable rows, but only about **7% phone overlap with the Google pilot**, so it is far thinner for beverage distributors. Not good enough alone.
- Business-registry industry code (VSIC 4633, wholesale of beverages) is not proof of an actual beverage distributor (companies register many codes). Idea left: use the registry only to verify and enrich candidates found elsewhere (tax code, legal name, status), not to discover them. masothue access from the sandbox and its terms were not checked.

**Ideas for when the owner returns:** (1) ask Google in writing if a real US entity may use Places for Vietnam business data; (2) a "thin but clean, cited" list from Overture + registry + the company lists KIRA already builds in the reports' "Who's who" chapter (`industry_players`); (3) another provider the owner has in mind. Evidence tiers worth keeping in any list: confirmed / likely / unverified, with a source link and a check date per row. Do not sell data from a source whose terms forbid storing or reselling it.
